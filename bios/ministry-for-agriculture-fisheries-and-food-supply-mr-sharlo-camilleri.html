Permanent Secretary — Ministry for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Supply

Mr Sharlo Camilleri

Ministry for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Supply

Mr Sharlo Camilleri is Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Supply, appointed in June 2026. He was born on 20 February 1976, is married with two children, and graduated from the University of Malta with a Diploma in Agriculture, an M.Sc in Environmental Studies and Planning, and a B.Sc. (Hons) in Geography from the University of London.

He began as a student worker at the Department of Agriculture and joined the Public Service in September 1997, initially involved in research, monitoring programmes, consultancies and technical advice on agricultural policy. In August 2006 he joined the Agricultural and Rural Payment Agency (ARPA), where he held roles including Deputy Director Operations, Agency Head, Director for Plant Health and Director General for Rural Development.

He has served on the Planning Authority Board and the Consultative Committee for Agriculture, chaired the Agriculture Advisory Committee, served on the Selection Committee for Rural Development Programme projects, and acted as Spokesperson on the Special Committee on Agriculture in the European Union Council. He was appointed Permanent Secretary in the Ministry for Agriculture, Fisheries and Animal Rights in January 2020, retaining the post through subsequent ministry redesignations (including a period covering "Food" from November 2020) up to his current appointment at the renamed Ministry for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Supply in June 2026.
