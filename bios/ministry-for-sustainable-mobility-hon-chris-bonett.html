Hon. Dr Chris Bonett

Ministry for Sustainable Mobility

Hon. Dr Chris Bonett is a Maltese lawyer and politician who has served as Minister for Sustainable Mobility since the June 2026 Cabinet formation — the renamed continuation of the Transport, Infrastructure and Public Works portfolio he held from January 2024. He holds a Doctor of Laws from the University of Malta (2007) and a Master of Law in Sports Law from De Montfort University, Leicester (2014).

Before entering national politics, Dr Bonett served as Vice President and Head of the Legal and Football Governance Department of the Malta Football Association, as a UEFA Integrity Officer and member of UEFA's Control, Ethics and Disciplinary Body, and as Mayor of Gżira from 2007 to 2012, where he delivered projects including a Community Centre, the Gżira Public Library, and the Council of Europe Gardens embellishment. He also chaired the Sports Scholarships Board and represented Malta on the Council of Europe's Enlarged Partial Agreement on Sport (EPAS).

Elected to Parliament in 2022 (District 4), he was appointed Parliamentary Secretary for European Funds before taking over the Transport, Infrastructure and Public Works portfolio in January 2024, succeeding Aaron Farrugia, with organisations such as cycling advocacy group Rota welcoming his appointment and setting out priorities including safer, more sustainable mobility infrastructure. Following the June 2026 Cabinet formation the portfolio was renamed Sustainable Mobility, encompassing ports, maritime affairs, aviation and internal transport, under the Government's "Malta in Motion" initiative. He is married to Dr Maxilene Bonett and they have two children, Matteo and Luca.
