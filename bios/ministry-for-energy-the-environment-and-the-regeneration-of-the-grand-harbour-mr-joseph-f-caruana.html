Permanent Secretary — Ministry for Energy, the Environment and the Regeneration of the Grand Harbour

Mr Joseph F. Caruana

Ministry for Energy, the Environment and the Regeneration of the Grand Harbour · also Sustainable Development Network Board member; Chairman, Eco-Contribution Approving Body

Mr Joseph F. Caruana is Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Energy, the Environment and the Regeneration of the Grand Harbour. He was born in Marsaxlokk on 2 May 1961, graduated with a B.A. Honours in Public Administration from the University of Malta in 1985, and an Executive MBA with distinction in 2001.

He joined the Public Service as a student-worker in 1980, and in 1985 was appointed Principal Officer in charge of the Land Department's Revenue Collection function. In 1996 he became Senior Principal, additionally taking on the role of IT Projects Coordinator for the Land Department and Estate Management Department. In October 1999 he was appointed Director of the Estate Management Department within the Government Property Division, and in November 2008 became Director General Operations in the Office of the Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Infrastructure, Transport & Communications, also serving as a Director of the Grand Harbour Regeneration Corporation.

Mr Caruana was appointed Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for the Environment, Sustainable Development and Climate Change in July 2013, retaining the post through successive ministry redesignations — Environment, Climate Change and Planning (2020); Environment, Energy and Enterprise (2022); Environment, Energy and Regeneration of the Grand Harbour (2024); and Environment, Energy and Public Cleanliness (2025) — up to his current appointment at the renamed Ministry for Energy, the Environment and the Regeneration of the Grand Harbour in June 2026. He is a Sustainable Development Network Board member and Chairman of the Eco-Contribution Approving Body.
