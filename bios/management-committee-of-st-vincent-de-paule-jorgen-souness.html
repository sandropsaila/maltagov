CEO

Jorgen Souness

Management Committee of St Vincent De Paule
Chief Executive Officer of St Vincent de Paul Residence, Malta's largest long-term care facility for the elderly, appointed Acting CEO in October 2023, succeeding Dr Josianne Cutajar who moved to head the Occupational Health and Safety Authority (OHSA). A nurse by profession who rose to the grade of Charge Nurse, previously served as Director of Quality Assurance within the Health Ministry's Secondary Care Services, where he coordinated Malta's national COVID-19 vaccination programme surveillance. Also held administrative roles at Mater Dei Hospital and in planning the Paola Hub. As of April 2026, oversaw the tendering of a new 300-bed intermediate care facility at St Vincent de Paul, designed as a 30-year build-operate-maintain project to bridge acute hospital treatment and long-term residential care.
