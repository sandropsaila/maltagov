Permanent Secretary — Ministry for Foreign and European Affairs

Mr Christopher Cutajar

Ministry for Foreign and European Affairs (also covers International Trade)

Mr Christopher Cutajar is Permanent Secretary of the Ministry for Foreign and European Affairs, the senior public official within the Ministry, heading its administration and coordinating all its departments under Minister Chris Fearne. He was born in 1985 and joined the Public Service in 2011, serving as Executive Secretary for Local Councils and for the national Local Councils' Association (initially with the Fgura Local Council).

In his younger days he was a civil society activist, serving as leader of the General Workers' Union Youths, editor of a local journal, and Financial Officer on the Executive Committee of the National Youth Council.

In June 2016 he was appointed Permanent Secretary for Transport, Infrastructure and Capital Projects, a post he held for four years. During that period he coordinated major projects including the regeneration of Triton Square, a landmark at the entrance of Valletta, and served as Deputy Chairperson of the Maritime Security Committee.

He was appointed Permanent Secretary for Foreign and European Affairs in June 2020. During his tenure he has worked closely with Malta's Ministers for Foreign Affairs to draft Malta's Foreign Policy Strategy, launched in 2022, and subsequent documents including the Foreign Policy Statement 2025–2026 and the Regional Framework Policies. He coordinated Malta's multilateral efforts including its non-permanent membership of the UN Security Council (2023–2024), the Chairpersonship-in-Office of the OSCE in 2024 (culminating in the OSCE Ministerial Council held in Malta in December 2024), and Malta's Presidency of the Committee of Ministers of the Council of Europe from May to November 2025. From December 2024 to June 2026 he was also Permanent Secretary responsible for Tourism, when that policy area fell within the remit of the Ministry for Foreign Affairs and Tourism. He remains Permanent Secretary for Foreign and European Affairs as of mid-2026, confirmed by recent diplomatic meeting records into July 2026.

He holds an Honours Degree and an Executive Master of Business Administration from the University of Malta, and has studied the Law of the Sea at the Summer Academy of the International Tribunal for the Law of the Sea in Hamburg. He sits on the Governing Board of Administrators of the International Institute for Justice and the Rule of Law since 2020. He is married with one son and is fluent in Maltese, English and Italian, and conversant in Spanish.
