Chairman (ex officio, as Secretary to the Cabinet)

Ryan Spagnol

Internal Audit and Investigations Board
Ryan Spagnol is Secretary to the Cabinet within the Office of the Prime Minister, a role that carries ex officio chairmanship of the Internal Audit and Investigations Board (IAIB) under the Internal Audit and Financial Investigations Act (Chapter 461 of the Laws of Malta). The IAIB is appointed by the Prime Minister and oversees the Internal Audit and Investigations Department (IAID), which conducts internal audits, EU funds audits, and financial investigations across government. Spagnol joined the public administration in 2013 with the Malta-EU Steering and Action Committee, later working at Identity Malta and the Ministry for Home Affairs on migration policy, before becoming Secretary to the Cabinet. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce in Management and Public Policy and a Master of Arts in Public Policy Leadership from the University of Malta. This chairmanship was determined by tracing the statutory role rather than from a direct announcement naming him to the IAIB specifically — the Act ties the chairmanship to the Cabinet Secretary post, and Spagnol is confirmed as the current holder of that post via the Office of the Prime Minister's own contact page.
