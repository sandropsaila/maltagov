Chairman (ex officio, as Head of the Civil Service)

Tony Sultana

Senior Appointments Advisory Committee
Tony Sultana is Malta's Principal Permanent Secretary and Head of the Civil Service, a post that carries ex officio chairmanship of the Senior Appointments Advisory Committee, which advises on headship appointments across the public administration. He was appointed Principal Permanent Secretary on 1 June 2022 after a career including Executive Chairman of MITA, and was confirmed continuing in the role following the Cabinet formed after the 2026 general election. This chairmanship was determined by tracing the statutory ex-officio role (documented in a University of Malta dissertation on public administration leadership) to the current holder of the Head of Civil Service post, rather than from a direct announcement naming him to the Committee specifically.
