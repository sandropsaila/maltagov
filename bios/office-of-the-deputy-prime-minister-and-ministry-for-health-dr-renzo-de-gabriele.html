Permanent Secretary — Office of the Deputy Prime Minister and Ministry for Health

Dr Renzo De Gabriele

Office of the Deputy Prime Minister and Ministry for Health

Dr Renzo De Gabriele is Permanent Secretary at the Office of the Deputy Prime Minister and Ministry for Health, working under Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Health Ian Borg. He is a family doctor by profession with over thirty years of experience in the Malta Public Service. He graduated as a Doctor of Medicine and Surgery from the University of Malta in 1995, was awarded a Certificate in Diabetes from the Irish College of General Practitioners in 2008, and is a Specialist in Family Medicine and a Fellow of the Malta College of Family Doctors. He is also an expert trainer in family medicine, having delivered national and international training courses, and sits on the Executive Board of the European Academy of Teachers in General Practice and Family Medicine (EURACT).

In 2011, Dr De Gabriele was appointed Assistant Director Administration in the Primary HealthCare Department within the Ministry for Health, and in 2013 he became Chief Executive Officer of the Primary Healthcare Department, a post he held for four years. In 2019 he was appointed Chief Executive Officer of Active Ageing and Community Care, where he was instrumental in strengthening existing services and launching new ones — including the Telecare on the Move service, domiciliary phlebotomy, the nationwide free Silver T transport service for the elderly, community psychotherapy services for the elderly, motorised bed rental for homebound clients, the home helper of choice scheme, and domiciliary dietitian services. He also oversaw the opening of new active ageing and dementia activity centres and a programme of renovation of government residential homes, including Floriana Residential Home, and supported implementation of the National Strategic Policy for Active Ageing 2023–2030 and the National Dementia Strategy 2024–2031.

In 2023, Dr De Gabriele received the Best Manager Award at the National Worker of the Year Awards, and Active Ageing and Community Care under his leadership won three further national awards that year (Best Innovative Service for the Silver T service, Best Project for the Dar Padova project, and Best Unit for the Dementia Directorate).

In May 2025, Dr De Gabriele was appointed Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Health and Active Ageing, one of three new Permanent Secretary appointments announced that month (alongside Mario Borg and Ronald Mizzi), nominated by Principal Permanent Secretary Tony Sultana to the Public Service Commission and recommended to the President of Malta. Following the June 2026 Cabinet formation he was appointed Permanent Secretary at the Office of the Deputy Prime Minister and Ministry for Health, a post he continues to hold.
