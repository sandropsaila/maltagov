Board Member

Dr Alexander Attard

Older Persons Standards Authority
Board Member of the Older Persons Standards Authority and, per OPSA's own 2024 Annual Report, concurrently serves as Malta's Commissioner for the Elderly; appointment for a three-year term with effect from 5 December 2023, valid until 18 January 2027. Appointed Commissioner for the Elderly in December 2023, succeeding Professor Godfrey Laferla. Worked as a surgeon at the University of Malta and St Luke's Hospital from 1993, where he founded the autonomous Renal Transplantation and Dialysis Access Services, and introduced Live Donor Renal Transplantation, Carotid Endarterectomy, Distal Limb Salvage Procedure and Laparoscopic Endocrine Surgical Procedure to Malta. He authored an influential, widely-cited article on pain in a surgical context. He retired from public service shortly before taking up the Commissioner role.
