Permanent Secretary — Ministry for European Funds, Social Dialogue and Consumer Protection

Mr Jonathan Vassallo

Ministry for European Funds, Social Dialogue and Consumer Protection

Mr Jonathan Vassallo is Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for European Funds, Social Dialogue and Consumer Protection. He began his Public Service career in 2006 as a Programme Manager within the Planning and Priorities Coordination Division (PPCD), responsible for financial control of the Single Programming Document (SPD) 2004–2006 and Cohesion Fund projects. He was later appointed Senior Manager within the PPCD and, in 2011, Director General, tasked with ensuring efficient absorption and management of EU and bilateral funds across Government departments, authorities and agencies.

During his tenure as Director General he led the closure of the 2004–2006 SPD and the 2007–2013 Cohesion Policy Operational Programmes, and spearheaded the launch of the 2014–2020 Operational Programmes, as well as playing a pivotal role in establishing Malta's first Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP).

In September 2022 Mr Vassallo was appointed Permanent Secretary responsible for EU Funds in Malta. By 2024 his portfolio had expanded to include Social Dialogue, Industrial and Employment Relations, Equality and Reforms, in which capacity he led Malta's delegation to the UN Human Rights Committee's review of the country's human rights practices. From May 2025 he served as Permanent Secretary within the Ministry for European Funds and the Implementation of the Electoral Programme, and in June 2026 was appointed to his current role at the Ministry for European Funds, Social Dialogue and Consumer Protection. He serves on various supervisory boards overseeing EU-funded financial instruments, and holds a degree in Public and Private Sector Management, specialising in Public Policy, from the University of Malta.
