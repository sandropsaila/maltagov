Principal Permanent Secretary

Tony Sultana

Internal Audit and Investigations Board
Office of the Prime Minister (OPM)
Tony Sultana is Malta's Principal Permanent Secretary and Head of the Civil Service, appointed 1 June 2022 after a career including Executive Chairman of MITA. He was confirmed continuing in the role following the Cabinet formed after the June 2026 general election.
Why included on this entity's record
Included because his post is institutionally adjacent to this Board's ex-officio chairmanship. The governing Act ties the IAIB chair specifically to the Secretary to the Cabinet post; through 2 September 2022 the same person (Mario Cutajar) held both the Secretary to the Cabinet and Principal Permanent Secretary titles simultaneously. The two posts have since been separated -- Sultana holds Principal Permanent Secretary, while Ryan Spagnol holds Secretary to the Cabinet and is the Board's actual current ex-officio chair.
