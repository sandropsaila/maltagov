Chairperson

Mr Michael Grech

Gozo Regional Development Authority
Chairperson of the Gozo Regional Development Authority (GRDA). Has worked in financial services for over four decades, starting his career at Mid-Med Bank Ltd before setting up his own private company. Currently a Director on the Board of Malta Enterprise, Director on the Board of JobsPlus, and Member of the Supervisory Council of the Malta Community Chest Fund; also Chairman of the Victoria Scout Group. Previously served as President of the Gozo Business Chamber (4 years), Chairman of the Gozo Regional Committee (4 years), Member of the Malta Council for Economic and Social Development (4 years), and Senior Vice President of INSULEUR, the association of island-region chambers of commerce of the EU.
