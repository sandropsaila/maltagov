Hon. Silvio Schembri

Ministry for the Economy, Technology and Strategic Projects

Hon. Silvio Schembri is a Maltese politician and economist who has served as Minister for the Economy (variously titled Economy, Enterprise and Strategic Projects, and since June 2026 the Economy, Technology and Strategic Projects) since January 2020, one of the ministers Prime Minister Robert Abela retained in their previous portfolio following the 2026 Cabinet formation. He was born on 21 May 1985 in Luqa, where his father John Schembri has served as Mayor since 2001. He holds a Bachelor of Honours in Economics and a Master's degree in Economics, both from the University of Malta, and has lectured in Economics there.

He began his career in a private bank before becoming Supervisor Economist at the Central Bank of Malta. First elected to Parliament in March 2013, he served as Parliamentary Secretary for Financial Services, Digital Economy and Innovation, during which he led Malta's first Artificial Intelligence Strategy, launched the AI Certification Programme, and helped establish Malta's blockchain regulatory framework, part of the push to brand Malta the "Blockchain Island." He also chaired the Responsible Gaming Foundation Malta on a voluntary basis and was a founding member of the Gal Xlokk foundation.

As Economy Minister, his portfolio has included the Malta Freeport Corporation, Bank of Valletta, Malta Government Investments Ltd, the Malta Information Technology Agency, the National Blockchain Strategy Foundation and the Malta Gaming Authority, and he played a central role in Malta's COVID-19 economic regeneration plan. He continues to lead work on Malta Vision 2050, the Government's long-term national strategy, and represents Malta at international fora including SiGMA and the EU-Startups Summit.
