CEO

Mr Kurt Formosa

Community Malta Agency
Chief Executive Officer of Community Malta Agency (Aġenzija Komunità Malta), the government agency administering all Maltese citizenship-related matters, appointed August 2025. Previously spent eight years with the Malta EU Steering and Action Committee (MEUSAC), latterly as Director, coordinating EU policy and civil society engagement. Worked at PwC Malta as a Manager on digital strategy and transformation. Brings sports administration experience, having served as Chief Football Officer at the Malta Football Association and Chief Executive of the 'Inħobb il-Football' Foundation.
