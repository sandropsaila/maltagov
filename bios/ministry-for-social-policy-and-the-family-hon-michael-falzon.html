Hon. Dr Michael Falzon

Ministry for Social Policy and the Family

Hon. Dr Michael Falzon is a Maltese politician who has served as Minister for Social Policy and the Family (previously titled Social Policy and Children's Rights) since 2020, retained in the role following the June 2026 Cabinet formation. He was born on 16 November 1961 in Sliema. He joined the Bank of Valletta as a clerk in 1979 before reading law at the University of Malta, and holds a Doctor of Laws degree as well as ACIB banking qualifications.

He was first elected to Parliament in the March 2008 general election, representing the 9th and 10th electoral divisions, and served as Deputy Leader of the Labour Party for Party Affairs from 2003 to 2008, prior to his own parliamentary election. His ministerial portfolio has covered social security, children's rights and family policy, and he has represented Malta at Council of Europe engagements on social policy. He is married to Anna (née Lia) and has two children, Martina and Nathaniel.
