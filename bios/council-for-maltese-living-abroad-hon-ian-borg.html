Deputy Prime Minister & Minister for Health

Hon. Ian Borg

Office of the Deputy Prime Minister and Ministry for Health · also Chairperson (ex officio), Council for Maltese Living Abroad

Hon. Ian Borg is a Maltese politician who has served as Deputy Prime Minister of Malta since September 2024 and, following the June 2026 Cabinet formation, as Minister for Health — a move Prime Minister Robert Abela described as reflecting the renewed importance of a sector under scrutiny over hospital waiting times. He was born on 28 February 1986 in Pietà and raised in Dingli. He graduated from the University of Malta in 2012 with a Doctorate in Laws, a Diploma in Public Notarial Practice and a Bachelor's Degree in Law.

Mr Borg was first elected Mayor of Dingli in 2005 at the age of 19, and has served as a Member of Parliament since March 2013. He was Parliamentary Secretary for the EU Presidency 2017 and EU Funds from 2013 to 2017, then Minister for Transport, Infrastructure and Capital Projects from June 2017 to March 2022. In March 2022 he was appointed Minister for Foreign and European Affairs and Trade, a portfolio he held until the June 2026 Cabinet formation, when it passed to Chris Fearne. During his tenure at Foreign Affairs he served as President of the Committee of Ministers of the Council of Europe (May–November 2025). He became Deputy Leader of the Labour Party for Parliamentary Affairs in May 2024, succeeding Chris Fearne, and Deputy Prime Minister that September.

Mr Borg also serves as Chairperson (ex officio) of the Council for Maltese Living Abroad, a role held concurrently with his ministerial portfolio; he presided over the Council's 17th Annual Meeting in October 2025, announcing a new Working Holiday scheme for youths of Maltese descent launching January 2026. He is married to Rachelle Borg Dingli and they have one daughter.
