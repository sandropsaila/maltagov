CEO

Kenneth Farrugia

Malta Financial Services Authority (MFSA)
Certified Public Accountant, fellow of the Malta Institute of Accountants; holds a Bachelor of Accountancy (Hons) and an MA in Business Ethics. Held audit roles in private practice and the public sector. Director General, Internal Audit and Investigations Department (IAID) for nine years to 2017. Director of the FIAU for six years to April 2023, leading major AML/CFT reforms. Appointed MFSA CEO in April 2023, succeeding acting CEO Michelle Mizzi Buontempo. Also serves as Chairman of the FIAU.
