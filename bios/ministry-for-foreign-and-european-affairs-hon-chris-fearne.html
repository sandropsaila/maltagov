Hon. Chris Fearne

Ministry for Foreign and European Affairs

Hon. Chris Fearne is a Maltese physician and politician who has served as Minister for Foreign and European Affairs since the June 2026 Cabinet formation, having returned to Cabinet after the May 2026 general election. He was born on 12 March 1963 in Attard and educated at St Aloysius' College and the University of Malta, qualifying in medicine (MD, FRCS(Ed)).

Mr Fearne was appointed Parliamentary Secretary for Health in April 2014 and Minister for Health in April 2016, a post he held for close to eight years. In July 2017 he was elected Deputy Leader of the Labour Party for Parliamentary Affairs, becoming Deputy Prime Minister of Malta, a role he held until May 2024. From January to May 2024 he additionally held the Ministry for European Funds, Social Dialogue and Consumer Protection. He resigned from Cabinet in 2024 after being charged in connection with the Vitals/Steward hospitals concession case, alongside former Prime Minister Joseph Muscat and others; he maintains his innocence and the case remains before the courts.

Following the Labour Party's re-election in the 30 May 2026 general election, Prime Minister Robert Abela reappointed Mr Fearne to Cabinet as Minister for Foreign and European Affairs, taking over the portfolio previously held by Ian Borg (who moved to the Ministry for Health). Since taking office, Mr Fearne has represented Malta at the EU Foreign Affairs Council, chaired the Commonwealth Ministerial Action Group (CMAG), and engaged in bilateral diplomacy including with the UK, US, India and Council of Europe partners. He is married to Astrid and has three children.
