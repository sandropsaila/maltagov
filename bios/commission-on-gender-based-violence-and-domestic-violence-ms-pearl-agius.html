Commission on Gender-Based Violence and Domestic Violence
Board Secretary of the Commission on Gender-Based Violence and Domestic Violence since June 2022. A warranted lawyer (admitted to the bar 2023) currently working as in-house legal counsel for WasteServ Malta Limited; previously spent six years (2017-2023) working in a local TV/radio newsroom.
