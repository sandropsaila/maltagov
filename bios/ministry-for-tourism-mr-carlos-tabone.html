Permanent Secretary — Ministry for Tourism & Ministry for Arts, Culture and National Heritage

Mr Carlos Tabone

Ministry for Tourism · also Permanent Secretary, Ministry for Arts, Culture and National Heritage

Mr Carlos Tabone is Permanent Secretary jointly for the Ministry for Tourism and the Ministry for Arts, Culture and National Heritage — one Permanent Secretary overseeing both Ministries following the June 2026 Cabinet formation. He was born in 1986 and started his career in the Public Service in 2009 as an EU Fund Officer at the Paying Agency, responsible for managing European funds under the Common Agricultural Policy programme. He graduated from the University of Malta with a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Social Policy in 2008 and an Executive Master of Business Administration in 2012.

In 2012, he was appointed Senior Manager at the Paying Agency, running the Accounts Division. In 2015 he moved to the Office of the Principal Permanent Secretary as Senior Manager, and in 2018 was appointed Head (Implementation) in the Prime Minister's Office while continuing to coordinate with the Office of the Principal Permanent Secretary. In 2020 he was appointed Director (Management Support) to the Office of the Principal Permanent Secretary.

In April 2022, Mr Tabone was appointed Permanent Secretary with responsibility for the Ministry for Public Works and Planning — one of three new Permanent Secretary appointments made that month by President George Vella on the recommendation of Principal Permanent Secretary Mario Cutajar, alongside Joseph Chetcuti (Health) and Emanuel Psaila (Home Affairs). In January 2024 he was entrusted with the Ministry for Lands and the Implementation of the Electoral Programme, and from May 2025 he served as Permanent Secretary (Equality, Reforms and Social Dialogue) within the Office of the Prime Minister.

In June 2026, Mr Tabone was appointed Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Tourism and the Ministry for Arts, Culture and National Heritage. He is married and has two children.
