Board Member

David Curmi

Malta Tourism Authority (MTA)
Financial services professional and corporate executive with 30+ years senior leadership experience. Former CEO of MAPFRE MSV Life plc; Director of MAPFRE Middlesea plc, International Hotel Investments plc (Corinthia Group), MIDI plc, and Plaza Centres plc. Former President of the Malta Chamber of Commerce, Enterprise and Industry. First Chairman of the Board of Governors of the National Development and Social Fund (NDSF) and of Trade Malta Ltd. Member of the Board of the Doctoral School, University of Malta. Executive Chairman of Air Malta plc (from c. Nov 2021) through to its 2024 restructuring into KM Malta Airlines, where he continued as Executive Chairman on an annual package of €260,000 (contract signed 1 Dec 2023, confirmed via FOI request, The Shift News, July 2025). Chartered Insurer, Associate of the Chartered Insurance Institute (UK). Member of the MTA Board.
