CEO

George Gregory

Malta Enterprise
Chartered accountant with over 30 years in the profession. Holds an ACCA (Association of Chartered Certified Accountants) qualification (completed 1990–1994) and earlier worked at Ernst & Young Malta and at MSD & Co, the Maltese representative firm of Arthur Andersen, before rising to partner and then Managing Partner roles at top-tier accountancy firms. Joined RSM Malta as Managing Partner at the start of 2021, leading the firm through the COVID-19 pandemic and post-pandemic transformation, a sustained period of client and team growth, and a series of collaboration agreements aimed at building the RSM brand locally. Across his career he led numerous high-profile engagements spanning acquisitions, restructuring, and commercial and tax advisory work, and has been described by colleagues as “action oriented,” a “fast thinker” and a “critical mind.” On 12 August 2024, the same day he informed RSM Malta's partners of his decision to retire from the firm, the Malta Enterprise board appointed him CEO of the agency, succeeding Kurt Farrugia, who moved to lead Transport Malta; Gregory took up the post on 9 September 2024, immediately beginning the process of severing all involvement with RSM Malta. As CEO, he is responsible for steering Malta Enterprise's investment-promotion strategy, supporting local enterprise growth and enhancing the country's economic competitiveness. In an April 2026 interview, he described Malta as increasingly well-positioned to focus on higher-quality, higher-innovation foreign investment rather than pure volume, framing the agency's role within the government's long-term Vision 2050 strategy.
