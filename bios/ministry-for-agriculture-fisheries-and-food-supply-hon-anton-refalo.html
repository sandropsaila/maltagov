Hon. Dr Anton Refalo

Ministry for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Supply

Hon. Dr Anton Refalo is a Maltese politician from Gozo who has served as Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Supply (previously titled Animal Rights) since January 2020, retained in the role following the June 2026 Cabinet formation. He was born in 1956 in Gozo and has contested every general election since 1987, always being elected on the first count — one of Labour's longest-serving parliamentarians, with over three decades as a Deputy.

He served as Parliamentary Secretary for Gozo from 1996 to 1998, and was appointed Minister for Gozo in 2013, a post he held until 2017. In May 2018 he was appointed Chairman of Heritage Malta. Following the January 2020 Cabinet reshuffle he was appointed Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Animal Rights, succeeding a brief tenure by Clint Camilleri.

As Agriculture Minister he has driven Malta's first National Food Policy and National Food Strategy for Resilient Food Systems, launched the National Ambassador for Organic and Sustainable Food post, and represents Malta at the FAO and at EU and Mediterranean (MED9) agriculture ministerial fora, including chairing the Party of European Socialists' Agriculture and Fisheries Committee. He has two children.
