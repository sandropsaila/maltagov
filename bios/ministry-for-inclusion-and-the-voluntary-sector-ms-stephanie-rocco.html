Permanent Secretary — Ministry for Inclusion and the Voluntary Sector

Ms Stephanie Rocco

Ministry for Inclusion and the Voluntary Sector

Ms Stephanie Rocco is Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Inclusion and the Voluntary Sector, appointed in June 2026 having previously served as Director General at the same Ministry. She has extensive experience in public administration, having joined the Malta Tourism Authority in 2008, where she worked in project management of EU-funded training programmes for the tourism and hospitality sector before becoming Internal HR Executive.

In 2015 she moved into the public service, joining the Office of the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry for Economy, Investment and Small Business as a Manager. Her career has centred on policy coordination, strategic planning, organisational management and stakeholder engagement, including work on performance monitoring, inter-ministerial coordination and delivery of complex programmes.

She holds an MBA specialising in Tourism from the Institut Universitaire Kurt Bösch in Switzerland and a Bachelor of Honours in Tourism Studies from the University of Malta, alongside further training in leadership, ethics, public procurement and public administration. She also serves on the Executive Board of the Malta Football Association and as General Secretary of the Youth Football Association.
