Hon. Dr Malcolm Paul Agius Galea

Ministry for Arts, Culture and National Heritage

Hon. Dr Malcolm Paul Agius Galea is a Maltese family doctor and politician who has served as Minister for Arts, Culture and National Heritage since the June 2026 Cabinet formation. He was born on 12 August 1982 and previously served as Mayor of Ħaż-Żebbuġ. He was first elected to Parliament in the 2022 general election from District 7.

Before his current appointment, Dr Agius Galea served as Parliamentary Secretary for Active Ageing within the Ministry of Health and Active Ageing. Following the June 2026 general election he was promoted to full ministerial rank, taking on the arts, culture, heritage and film policy portfolio, including responsibility for the Malta Film Commission and the Screen Support Scheme. He has described culture as a sector he has "always been drawn to," citing carnival, village festas and heritage as priorities. In July 2026, opposition MPs criticised the minister for delaying parliamentary questions on Film Commission finances.
