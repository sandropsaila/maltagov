CEO

Mr Leonid McKay

Transport Malta
Chief Executive of Transport Malta, appointed 3 July 2026, succeeding Kurt Farrugia. Previously Chief Executive of the Housing Authority from 2018, Executive Chairman of the Authority on the Responsible Use of Cannabis from 2022, then moved to JobsPlus before becoming Labour Party CEO in 2024, a post he held until this appointment. Also has a background as a director of Caritas Malta. Dr Stephanie Bonello was separately appointed to lead implementation of the government's 'Malta in Motion' mass transit plan, a proposed rail line connecting Valletta, the airport, St Paul's Bay, Mater Dei Hospital and Qormi with a projected cost of €2.8 billion.
