Board Member

George Micallef

Malta Tourism Authority (MTA)
RESOLVED (previously unconfirmed) — Board Member of the Malta Tourism Authority and Deputy Chairman of the Management Board of the Malta Tourism Observatory since its inception, confirmed via the Observatory's own “About Us” and speaker pages. A freelance tourism development consultant since 1987 with a lifelong career in the tourism industry, remaining actively involved in the hotel industry throughout; holds directorships on a number of companies and sits on various local and international tourism-governing boards. Has led projects in both the public and private sectors in Malta and overseas, served as a consultant to successive Ministers of Tourism, and has published articles and delivered conference papers on tourism development. At the Observatory's 2026 annual conference, he was slated to lead the “Accommodation & Product” strand of Malta's Tourism Strategy Review & Reset 2027-2035. Distinct from an unrelated former MHRA President and 2008-era MTA consultant of the same name involved in the Mistra Bay planning controversy — the timelines and roles do not clearly establish these as the same or different individuals, so no further detail from that episode is attributed here.
