Hon. Owen Bonnici

Ministry for Accommodation and Lands

Hon. Owen Bonnici is a Maltese politician who has served as Minister for Accommodation and Lands since the June 2026 Cabinet formation. He was born on 24 May 1980 in Żejtun and, prior to entering Parliament, served on the Marsascala Local Council as Councillor and later Deputy Mayor, and as an elected member of the Labour Executive Council (2004–2008).

Mr Bonnici was appointed Parliamentary Secretary for Justice in 2013, then Minister for Justice, Culture and Local Government from March 2014 to January 2020. He subsequently served as Minister for Education and Employment (2020), Minister for Research, Innovation and the Coordination of the Post-COVID-19 Strategy (2020–2022), and from March 2022 as Minister for the National Heritage, the Arts and Local Government, during which he inaugurated MICAS, Malta's first international contemporary art museum, in 2024. In May 2025 he took over the Lands portfolio.

Following the June 2026 Cabinet formation, Mr Bonnici was appointed Minister for Accommodation and Lands, with responsibility for housing and land management policy. He has described affordable housing and land management as among the biggest challenges facing Maltese society, with a priority of ensuring support reaches those seeking to enter the property market efficiently.
