Permanent Secretary — Ministry for Justice, Research and Innovation

Mr Johan Galea

Ministry for Justice, Research and Innovation

Mr Johan Galea has served as Permanent Secretary within the Justice sector continuously since December 2016, initially in the Ministry for Justice, Culture and Local Government. Following a ministry redesignation in January 2020, he continued in the Ministry for Justice, Equality and Governance, and from April 2022 in the Ministry for Justice. In February 2022 he additionally took on the role of Permanent Secretary for the Ministry for Transport, Infrastructure and Capital Projects, concurrently serving both ministries for nine months.

In January 2024 he was appointed Permanent Secretary for the Ministry for Justice and Reform of the Building and Construction Sector, and in June 2026 was appointed Permanent Secretary for the renamed Ministry for Justice, Research and Innovation.

Mr Galea joined the Public Service in 1991 in the teaching grade, later serving as Assistant Head of School and Head of School (2004–2009). In 2009 he was appointed Assistant Director in the Planning and Priorities Coordination Division within the Office of the Prime Minister. In 2012 he became Director of Corporate Services within the Ministry for Fair Competition, Small Business and Consumers, moving to the same role within the Ministry for European Affairs and Implementation of the Electoral Manifesto in 2013. In 2015 he was appointed Director of Corporate Affairs at Arts Council Malta. He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology and Sociology from the University of Malta, a Postgraduate Certificate in Education, and a Diploma in Educational Administration and Management.
