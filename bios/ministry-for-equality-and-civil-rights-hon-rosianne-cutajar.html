Hon. Rosianne Cutajar

Ministry for Equality and Civil Rights

Hon. Rosianne Cutajar is a Maltese politician who has served as Minister for Equality and Civil Rights since the June 2026 Cabinet formation, marking her return to Cabinet after several years on the backbench. She was born on 4 September 1988 in Pietà, holds a BA (Hons) and PGCE from the University of Malta, and served as Mayor of Qormi from 2012 to 2017 before her election to Parliament in June 2017.

She previously served as Parliamentary Secretary for Equality, Reforms and Good Governance (2020) and then Civil Rights and Reforms, within the Ministry for Justice, Equality and Governance, working on equality law, cannabis reform and Identity Malta. She resigned in February 2021 following reports concerning her association with Yorgen Fenech, the businessman charged in connection with the murder of journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia, and subsequently left the Labour Party after the leak of private communications between them. She rejoined Labour's parliamentary group in 2024 without issuing a public apology, and stood for re-election as an Independent-turned-Labour candidate.

Following the May 2026 general election, Prime Minister Robert Abela appointed her Minister for Equality and Civil Rights in a newly created standalone ministry, a portfolio that also covers minority rights, reforms and Malta's foreign communities. She has stated her first priority is reforming Malta's IVF law, and has driven forward the National Commission for Human Rights and Equality Bill, which a coalition of civil society organisations criticised in July 2026 for insufficient consultation. She is in a domestic partnership with Daniel Farrugia and has one child.
