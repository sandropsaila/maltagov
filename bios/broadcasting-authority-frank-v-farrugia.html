Chairman

Frank V. Farrugia

Broadcasting Authority
Began his career in the Engineering department at Rediffusion Malta Ltd. Served as President of the Malta Chamber of Commerce, Enterprise and Industry for a two-year term. Also served on the boards of the Malta Council for Economic and Social Development (MCESD), Malta Enterprise, Jobs Plus, and Employment Industrial Relations bodies. Appointed Chairman of the Broadcasting Authority in November 2019 by the President of Malta; confirmed still in the role as of March 2024 reporting.
