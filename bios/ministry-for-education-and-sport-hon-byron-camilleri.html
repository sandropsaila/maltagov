Hon. Dr Byron Camilleri

Ministry for Education and Sport

Hon. Dr Byron Camilleri is a Maltese lawyer and politician who has served as Minister for Education and Sport since the June 2026 Cabinet formation. He was born on 28 January 1988 and studied law at the University of Malta. He served as Mayor of Fgura from 2010 to 2017, and was elected to Parliament in the June 2017 general election, representing the Fourth District.

Mr Camilleri served as Government Whip from 2017 to 2020, before being appointed Minister for Home Affairs, National Security and Employment in January 2020. He was re-elected in the 2022 general election and reappointed to the Home Affairs portfolio, subsequently serving as Minister for Home Affairs, Security, Reforms and Equality, with responsibility for law enforcement, migration and civil protection during a period that included the September 2023 Maltese rescue mission response to the Derna floods disaster in Libya.

Following the May 2026 general election, Mr Camilleri was appointed Minister for Education and Sport, having made clear he did not wish to continue in his previous Home Affairs portfolio. He succeeded Clifton Grima, who moved to the Ministry for Justice, Research and Innovation. The Malta Football Association and the Malta Union of Teachers both welcomed his appointment. Since taking office he has emphasised continuity with the National Strategy for Education 2024–2030 and the education infrastructure investment programme, including visits to newly built schools. He is married to Salome (née Catania) and has one child.
