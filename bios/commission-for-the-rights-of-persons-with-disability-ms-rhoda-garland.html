Commissioner

Ms Rhoda Garland

Commission for the Rights of Persons with Disability
Commissioner for the Rights of Persons with Disability (CRPD), appointed October 2023 after serving as Acting Commissioner. Graduated with a Bachelor with Honours in Accounting and Finance and became a Management Accountant with the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants; worked for many years as a Management Accountant for a UK-based company before returning to Malta as an IT consultant. In 2014, began working as a researcher on Malta's first UN Convention report within the Parliamentary Secretariat for the Rights of Persons with Disability and Active Ageing. Completed a Masters in Disability Studies from the University of Malta in 2015, then joined the CRPD, becoming its Executive Director from 2016. Former President of the NGO Breaking Limits.
