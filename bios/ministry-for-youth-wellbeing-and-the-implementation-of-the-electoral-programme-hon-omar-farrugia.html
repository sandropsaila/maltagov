Hon. Omar Farrugia

Ministry for Youth, Wellbeing and the Implementation of the Electoral Programme

Hon. Omar Farrugia is a Maltese politician who, following the June 2026 Cabinet formation, became Malta's youngest-ever Cabinet minister at 31 years old. He is from Mqabba, where he was elected Mayor in 2019 at the age of 24. He was elected to Parliament from District 5 in the 2022 general election, and in January 2024 was appointed Parliamentary Secretary for Public Works within the Ministry for Transport, Infrastructure and Public Works — his first executive role.

Following Labour's re-election in the 30 May 2026 general election, in which he secured over 2,000 first-preference votes on his home district, Prime Minister Robert Abela appointed him to head the newly created Ministry for Youth, Wellbeing and the Implementation of the Electoral Programme, combining youth affairs, wellbeing policy and oversight of the Government's electoral manifesto delivery. He has stated that continued investment in understanding young people's aspirations is central to his portfolio, and has met with union representatives on issues including AI and digitalisation affecting young workers.
