Permanent Secretary — Ministry for Social Policy and the Family

Mr Mark Musù

Ministry for Social Policy and the Family · also Chairperson, Pensions Strategy Group; Deputy Chairperson, MCESD, Low Wage Commission and MGA Board

Mr Mark Musù is Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Social Policy and the Family, appointed in June 2026. He joined the Public Service in 1986, holding various positions within the Ministry for the Family, Children's Rights and Social Solidarity and the Department of Social Security. In December 2005 he was appointed Director responsible for EU & International Affairs within the Ministry for the Family and Social Solidarity, and in November 2008 became Director (Strategic Development and International Relations) in the Department of Social Security, followed by Director (Pensions Strategy) in November 2012. He has represented Malta in various EU social security and international committees.

In March 2013 he was appointed Permanent Secretary within the Ministry for the Family and Social Solidarity, and was reappointed in June 2017 within the renamed Ministry for the Family, Children's Rights and Social Solidarity. In November 2020 he became Permanent Secretary in the Ministry for Social Justice and Solidarity, the Family and Children's Rights, and simultaneously Permanent Secretary in the Ministry for Social Accommodation. In April 2022 he was appointed Permanent Secretary in the Ministry for Social Policy and Children's Rights, additionally covering the Ministry for Social and Affordable Accommodation (2022–2023) and the Ministry for Inclusion and the Voluntary Sector (July–December 2023) at various points.

Mr Musù presently chairs the Pensions Strategy Group and serves as Deputy Chairperson of the Malta Council for Economic and Social Development (MCESD), the Low Wage Commission, and the Board of Directors of the Malta Gaming Authority (MGA), as well as a member of the Sustainable Development Network Board and the Anti-Human Trafficking Committee. He graduated in Public Administration.
