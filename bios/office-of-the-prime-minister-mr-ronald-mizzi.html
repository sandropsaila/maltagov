Permanent Secretary — Office of the Prime Minister (Malta Vision 2050, Implementation and Coordination)

Mr Ronald Mizzi

Office of the Prime Minister
Source conflict flagged
Malta's public-service personnel directory (publicservice.gov.mt) describes Mr Mizzi as appointed Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Infrastructure, Planning and Employment in June 2026. However, multiple contemporaneous news reports dated after the June 2026 Cabinet formation — including an Office of the Prime Minister press release (22 April 2026) and a Shift News report (20 June 2026) explicitly noting he "remains" OPM Permanent Secretary — describe him as continuing to lead Malta Vision 2050 from within the Office of the Prime Minister. This register lists him under both entities pending clarification; readers should treat one of the two current-role claims as potentially outdated.

Mr Ronald Mizzi is a seasoned Maltese public servant with more than two decades of experience in economic planning, European affairs, public administration and strategic policy development. Since May 2025 he has led Malta Vision 2050, a landmark national foresight initiative establishing a long-term framework for Malta's economic, environmental and social development, coordinating engagement across government, business, civil society and youth stakeholders — continuing in this role into mid-2026 according to OPM and news sources, including work with the OECD on strategic-foresight capacity building announced in April 2026.

Between 2022 and 2024 he served as Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for the Economy, Enterprise and Strategic Projects, responsible for economic development, foreign direct investment, innovation, the digital economy and enterprise policy. Between 2017 and 2022 he served as Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Tourism, leading the sector through record growth and the COVID-19 pandemic disruption. Earlier, as Permanent Secretary for Energy and within the Office of the Prime Minister (2014–2017), he played a central role in energy sector reform. He holds a Master's degree in European Studies and a Bachelor's degree in International Relations from the University of Malta, and is originally from Żabbar.

Mr Mizzi has faced criminal charges in connection with the Vitals/Steward hospitals concession case; he denies wrongdoing and proceedings are ongoing, as separately reported by MaltaToday in connection with his 2025 appointment.
