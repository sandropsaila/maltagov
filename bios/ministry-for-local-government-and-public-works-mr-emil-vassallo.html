Permanent Secretary — Ministry for Local Government and Public Works

Mr Emil Vassallo

Ministry for Local Government and Public Works

Mr Emil Vassallo is Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Local Government and Public Works, appointed in June 2026. An educator by profession, he served in various roles in the Malta Public Service over 32 years, including teacher, adult educator, Head of School, College Principal and Director General within the Education Division.

For two and a half years prior to his Permanent Secretary appointment, he served as Director General within the Local Government Division, leading the team supporting Malta's 68 local councils and 6 regional councils. He holds a Bachelor's degree from the University of Malta, a Postgraduate Diploma in Leadership, and a Master of Science in ICT in Education. He has particular interests in training and professional development and the digitalisation of public services, and is an amateur distance runner who has completed marathon distances.
