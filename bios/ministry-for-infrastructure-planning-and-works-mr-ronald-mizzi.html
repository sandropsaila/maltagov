Permanent Secretary — Ministry for Infrastructure, Planning and Works

Mr Ronald Mizzi

Ministry for Infrastructure, Planning and Works

Mr Ronald Mizzi is a seasoned Maltese public servant with more than two decades of experience in economic planning, European affairs, public administration and strategic policy development. In June 2026 he was appointed Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Infrastructure, Planning and Employment (the Ministry's own materials use "Employment" rather than "Works" in its formal title). Prior to this appointment he served as Permanent Secretary at the Office of the Prime Minister, responsible for Malta Vision 2050, and for Implementation and Coordination.

A key milestone in his career was leading Malta Vision 2050, a landmark national foresight initiative establishing a long-term framework for Malta's economic, environmental and social development, coordinating engagement across government, business, civil society and youth stakeholders. Between 2022 and 2024 he served as Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for the Economy, Enterprise and Strategic Projects, responsible for economic development, foreign direct investment, innovation, the digital economy and enterprise policy. Between 2017 and 2022 he served as Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Tourism, leading the sector through record growth and the COVID-19 pandemic disruption, and initiating a shift toward quality-driven tourism, with responsibilities also spanning aviation, public-private partnerships, the film industry, consumer protection and external transport.

Earlier, as Permanent Secretary for Energy and within the Office of the Prime Minister (2014–2017), Mr Mizzi played a central role in energy sector reform, focusing on infrastructure investment, regulatory modernisation and public-private partnerships in energy and water policy. His early career was rooted in EU Structural and Cohesion Funds management. He holds a Master's degree in European Studies and a Bachelor's degree in International Relations from the University of Malta, and is originally from Żabbar.
