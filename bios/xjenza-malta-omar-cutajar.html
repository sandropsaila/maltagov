Senior Director

Omar Cutajar

Xjenza Malta
Justice, Research and Innovation (MJRI)
Omar Cutajar is Senior Director for Strategy, Research and Technical Affairs at Xjenza Malta, confirmed as current in the Authority's official Management Team page (updated April 2026) and in numerous 2025–2026 Xjenza Malta press releases and event coverage, including his role addressing the April 2026 ESA Industry Days hosted by Xjenza Malta in Malta. He has led Xjenza Malta's Space Unit initiatives, including the Authority's 2024 membership of the International Astronautical Federation and its 2024 accession to the European Space Agency's PECS (Plan for European Cooperating States) programme, securing an annual national budgetary envelope of roughly €1.5 million over five years. Earlier in his career, he chaired Malta's Space Task Force, which drafted the country's first National Space Strategy (2022–2027), and served as Malta's representative within the EU Council of Ministers for Research, Innovation and Space, where he chaired the Research Working Party during Malta's 2017 EU Council Presidency and helped secure agreement establishing the PRIMA (Partnership for Research & Innovation in the Mediterranean Area) programme.
Source conflict flagged
A bio hosted on the website of Time&Place Consulting lists Omar Cutajar as a "Senior Consultant" there, describing his Xjenza Malta role in past tense under a section titled "Recent Roles and Outgoing Impact." This conflicts with Xjenza Malta's own site (last updated 2 April 2026) and multiple 2026 press releases and event write-ups (through at least late April 2026) that continue to identify him as a current Senior Director. Given the volume and recency of the Xjenza Malta-sourced material, this entry follows the Authority's own listing as the more reliable current source, but the conflicting consulting-firm bio is noted here rather than silently discarded.
