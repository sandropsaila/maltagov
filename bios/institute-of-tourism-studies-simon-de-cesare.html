Board Member

Simon De Cesare

Institute of Tourism Studies

Simon De Cesare is a member of the ITS Board of Governors, serving alongside Chairperson Edward Zammit and Deputy Chairperson Michelle Muscat Portelli. He is also Chief Executive Officer of the Eden Leisure Group, one of Malta's largest hospitality, leisure and entertainment groups, a role he has held since 2018.

He holds a BSc in Business Administration from Bentley University (Boston) and an MBA in strategy development and marketing from the Peter Drucker School of Management at Claremont Graduate University (California). De Cesare joined the Eden Leisure Group in April 2000 as Business Administration Manager, was appointed Company Secretary across the Group's boards in 2006 and a director of several subsidiary boards, became Director of Operations in 2011, and was named Group CEO in 2018. He also serves as a director of Eden Finance plc, listed on the Malta Stock Exchange.

Beyond ITS and Eden Leisure, De Cesare is a member of the Council of the Malta Hotels and Restaurants Association (MHRA) and Vice-President of the Malta Business Bureau (MBB), the EU-business advisory office of the Malta Chamber of Commerce, Enterprise and Industry and the MHRA — a role he took up after serving as MBB President from 2018 to 2021, when he was succeeded by Alison Mizzi.
