Hon. Dr Glenn Bedingfield

Ministry for Home Affairs and Security

Hon. Dr Glenn Bedingfield is a Maltese politician who was appointed Minister for Home Affairs and Security following the June 2026 Cabinet formation — a major promotion from a junior public cleanliness brief to one of Cabinet's most sensitive portfolios. He was born on 15 November 1974 in Pietà. He began his career as a journalist at the Labour-aligned Super 1 newsroom, receiving a Best Journalist award in 2002, and authored books on political-criminal links and party leader profiles. He joined the Labour Party as a teenager in 1988, later serving as general secretary of its youth forum (1996–1998) and on its national executive from 1995.

He was first elected to the European Parliament via casual election in 2008, serving briefly on the Committee on Budgets and the Committee on Regional Development. He was elected to the Parliament of Malta in June 2017, served as Government Whip from 2020 to 2022, and was appointed Parliamentary Secretary for Public Cleanliness in January 2024, working under the Ministry for the Environment, Energy and Public Cleanliness.

His June 2026 appointment as Home Affairs Minister drew criticism from the Daphne Caruana Galizia Foundation, which cited findings from the public inquiry into the journalist's 2017 assassination that referenced a blog Mr Bedingfield ran while working within the Office of the Prime Minister as one of the strongest outlets for a campaign against her. As Minister, his portfolio covers defence, the armed forces, law enforcement, immigration, aviation security, correctional and detention services, and civil protection; he has engaged with the EU Asylum Agency (EUAA) on migration policy and visited rehabilitation services for released prisoners as his first official engagement.
