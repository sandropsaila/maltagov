Chairperson

Matthew Farrugia

Consultative Committee on Sustainable Mobility
Sustainable Mobility (MSM)
Matthew Farrugia is Chairperson of the Consultative Committee on Sustainable Mobility, appointed 12 July 2025 following a meeting with then-Transport Minister Chris Bonett. Alongside the chairmanship, he also holds the parallel role of Ambassador for Sustainable Mobility, tasked with promoting alternative mobility, organising meetings and workshops, and safeguarding the interests of vulnerable citizens in transport planning. Before this appointment, he served as a policy officer at Rota.mt, a Maltese cycling advocacy NGO, which publicly welcomed his appointment to the chairmanship. The Committee advises on sustainable transport projects, promotes innovation and inclusion in urban mobility planning, and recommends measures to improve air quality and transport access, forming part of the government's wider 'Reshaping Our Mobility' 18-month programme.
