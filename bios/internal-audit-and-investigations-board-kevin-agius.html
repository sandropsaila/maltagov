Director General, IAID

Kevin Agius

Internal Audit and Investigations Board
Office of the Prime Minister (OPM)
Kevin Agius is Director General of the Internal Audit and Investigations Department (IAID), appointed 2 January 2024. IAID conducts internal audits, EU funds audits, and financial investigations across Malta's public administration. He runs the Department's day-to-day operations and reports to the Internal Audit and Investigations Board (IAIB), the statutory oversight body established under the Internal Audit and Financial Investigations Act (Chapter 461) -- he does not chair the Board itself, which is chaired ex officio by the Secretary to the Cabinet. He is included on this register because he is the most senior official within the entity the Board exists to oversee.
