CEO

Richard Bilocca

WasteServ (Malta) Ltd.
Chief Executive Officer of WasteServ (Malta) Ltd, the national waste management agency, having previously served two-and-a-half years as CEO of the Water Services Corporation (since October 2017). Holds a Master's in Environmental Sciences and Natural Resource Management from the University of Iceland; worked at the Icelandic Institute of Sustainable Development and for seven years at the Permanent Representation of Malta to the EU, including chairing an EU Council Working Party during Slovakia's 2016 EU Council Presidency. Under his WSC tenure, achieved a reverse osmosis plant in Gozo, an 8% reduction in energy costs, and a major campaign against water losses and theft.
