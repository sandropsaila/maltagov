Hon. Dr Miriam Dalli

Ministry for Energy, the Environment and the Regeneration of the Grand Harbour

Hon. Dr Miriam Dalli is a Maltese politician, lawyer and former journalist and MEP who has served as Minister for the Environment and Energy (under several portfolio names) since November 2020, retained in the role following the June 2026 Cabinet formation. She was born on 19 May 1976 in St Julian's / Ħal Safi. She holds a Bachelor of Laws, a Bachelor in Communications, a Master's in European Studies, and an Executive MBA, all from the University of Malta.

She began her career in journalism, working over 15 years in the sector and becoming the first woman to serve as news editor of a Maltese TV station, presenting current-affairs programmes including TRIP, TEAM and TX. She served as a Member of the European Parliament from 2014 to 2020, where she chaired the S&D Group's Environment Committee, was ranked among the most influential MEPs on environmental policy by VoteWatch Europe, and won the MEP Award for Public Health in 2017.

In 2020 Prime Minister Robert Abela invited her to join the national Parliament, and she was appointed Minister for Energy, Enterprise and Sustainable Development, directing Malta's COVID-19 economic response including the Wage Supplement and Rent and Energy Subsidies. Following the March 2022 election she became Minister for the Environment, Energy and Enterprise, and from January 2024 held the Environment, Energy and Public Cleanliness portfolio (later renamed to include Regeneration of the Grand Harbour). She has represented Malta at UNFCCC COP29 and other international climate fora, and continues to lead Malta's carbon-neutrality-by-2050 agenda, open-space creation programme, and energy price-stability policy. She is married to John (son of former EU Commissioner Karmenu Vella) and they have two sons, Jack and Kane.
