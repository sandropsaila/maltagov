Board Member

Mr Anthony Bezzina

Gozo Regional Development Authority
Member of the Gozo Regional Development Authority (GRDA) board. Spent 43 years in banking (Mid-Med Bank 1975-1998, Lombard Bank 1998-2017), including six years as Lombard's Chief Officer for Gozo affairs; elected Associate of the Chartered Institute of Bankers of London in 1981. Part-time banking lecturer at Sir Michelangelo Refalo Sixth Form School (1988-2001). Since 2018 a non-executive director at Michael Grech Financial Investment Services, and Office Manager at the Gozo Curia. Active on several community and religious councils including the Gozo Business Chamber and Fundazzjoni Il-Hajja f'Ghawdex.
