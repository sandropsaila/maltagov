Chairman

Leo Brincat

Malta Development Bank
Chairman of the Malta Development Bank, appointed by the Minister for Finance and Employment, succeeding outgoing Chairman Prof Josef Bonnici. A former banker and former government minister who held the Finance and Commerce portfolio as well as the Sustainable Development, Climate Change and Environment portfolio; also served as Malta's Member at the European Court of Auditors and as Chairman of the Public Accounts Committee. Fellow of the Institute of Financial Services and Associate of the Chartered Institute of Bankers; Officer of the National Order of Merit (Malta).
