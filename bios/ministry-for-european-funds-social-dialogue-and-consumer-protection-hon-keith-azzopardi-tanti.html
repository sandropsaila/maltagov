Hon. Keith Azzopardi Tanti

Ministry for European Funds, Social Dialogue and Consumer Protection

Hon. Keith Azzopardi Tanti is a Maltese politician who was appointed Minister for European Funds, Social Dialogue and Consumer Protection following the June 2026 Cabinet formation, a significant promotion from Parliamentary Secretary. He was born on 17 March 1984 and elected to Parliament representing District 1 in the 2022 general election.

Before his ministerial appointment he served as Parliamentary Secretary for Youth, Research and Innovation within the Ministry for Education, Sport, Youth, Research and Innovation, during which he oversaw youth civic-engagement programmes reaching thousands of young people, addressed the World Government Summit 2024 in the UAE, and helped establish the Youth Advisory Forum, a national consultative framework connecting young people aged 16–35 directly with the Prime Minister.

As Minister, he has addressed the International Labour Conference on AI, platform work and social dialogue, and met with trade unions including the General Workers' Union on issues facing Maltese workers in an increasingly digital economy. His portfolio spans EU fund absorption, industrial and employment relations, and consumer protection policy.
