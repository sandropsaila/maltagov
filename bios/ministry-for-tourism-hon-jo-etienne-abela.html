Hon. Dr Jo Etienne Abela

Ministry for Tourism

Hon. Dr Jo Etienne Abela is a Maltese surgeon and politician who has served as Minister for Tourism since the June 2026 Cabinet formation. He was born in Gozo on 29 November 1975 and attended Sir Arturo Mercieca Primary School, Ninu Cremona Junior Lyceum, and sixth form at Sir Michelangelo Refalo, all in Gozo. He studied medicine and surgery at the University of Malta from 1993 to 1999, then worked as a medical doctor at St Luke's Hospital before moving to Glasgow, Scotland in 2003 to specialise in surgery, becoming the youngest consultant surgeon in the country and sub-specialising in oesophageal and pancreatic surgery.

He returned to Malta in 2012 and from 2013 worked at Mater Dei Hospital and Gozo Hospital, also serving as a visiting lecturer at the University of Malta. He held administrative posts including Deputy Head of the Department of Surgery and Lead Clinician for the High Dependency Unit, endoscopy and pancreatic disease, and is an examiner for the Royal College of Surgeons in Edinburgh and the Union Européenne des Médecins Spécialistes (UEMS).

Dr Abela was elected to Parliament in the March 2022 general election and appointed Minister for Active Ageing. In January 2024 the Health portfolio was added, and he served as Minister for Health and Active Ageing until the June 2026 Cabinet formation, when Prime Minister Robert Abela moved him to the Ministry for Tourism — a portfolio previously held by Ian Borg — while Deputy Prime Minister Ian Borg took over Health. Announcing the reshuffle, the Prime Minister said the changes were designed to make the best use of the skills available within his Cabinet. He lives in Sliema, is married, and has three children.
