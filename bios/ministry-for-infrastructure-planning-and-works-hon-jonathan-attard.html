Hon. Dr Jonathan Attard

Ministry for Infrastructure, Planning and Works

Hon. Dr Jonathan Attard is a Maltese lawyer and former journalist who has served as Minister for Infrastructure, Planning and Employment (also referred to as Planning and Works) since the June 2026 Cabinet formation. He was born on 7 May 1984 in Tarxien. He graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Communications and Social Studies in 2004, a Bachelor of Laws in 2011, a Diploma of Notary Public, and a Doctor of Laws in 2013, and was called to the Bar in 2015. His doctoral research examined increased accountability for Members of Parliament.

Before entering Cabinet, Dr Attard worked as a lawyer in the Maltese Courts of Justice specialising in Civil, Public, Property, Planning and Corporate Law, and served as Communications Coordinator and liaison officer between the Ministry for the Economy, Investment and Small Business and Malta Enterprise. He was elected to Parliament in July 2021 and appointed Minister for Justice and Reform of the Construction Sector in March 2022, with the construction sector reform remit added in 2023. In this role he oversaw amendments to magisterial inquiry law, the establishment of the Commissioner for Standards in the Judiciary, a new Occupational Health and Safety framework, and mason licensing reforms.

Following the June 2026 Cabinet formation, Dr Attard was given responsibility for planning, infrastructure and employment — one of Prime Minister Robert Abela's most trusted ministers, tasked with steering a reform of Malta's planning laws. His portfolio includes development planning, infrastructural projects, roads, employment and training, construction, occupational health and safety, national identity management, and legal migration.
