Chairman

Dr Paul Cachia

Malta Arbitration Centre
Chairman of the Board of Governors of the Malta Arbitration Centre, appointed 2019 after previously serving as a Board member. An advocate specialising in cross-border litigation, civil, commercial, constitutional and human rights litigation in the Courts of Malta. Holds a first-class B.A. in Legal & Humanistic Studies, Diploma of Notary Public, LL.D. (2003), and a Magister Juris and M.Phil from the University of Oxford as a Chevening Scholar. Worked as a lawyer with the European Commission in Brussels (2008-2010); chaired the Civil Justice Working Parties during Malta's EU Council Presidency and continues to represent Malta on the Council's Civil Law Working Party. Former visiting lecturer at the University of Malta on the Law of Tort and Private International Law.
