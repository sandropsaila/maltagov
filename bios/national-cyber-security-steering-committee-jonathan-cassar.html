Chairman

Jonathan Cassar

National Cyber Security Steering Committee
Office of the Prime Minister (OPM)
Jonathan Cassar serves as MITA's Chief Information Security Officer, leading the Cybersecurity Services Department and heading the National Cybersecurity Coordination Centre. With over 25 years of experience progressing from software development to senior leadership, he has aligned MITA's security posture with European frameworks such as NIS2 and the Cybersecurity Act, while driving ISO 27001 and ISO 22301 standards. He chairs the National Cyber Security Steering Committee and oversees the National Security Operations Centre. The Committee received the Public Service Award in the Digital Category, confirmed via MITA's official LinkedIn page.
