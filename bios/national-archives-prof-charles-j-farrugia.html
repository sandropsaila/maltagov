CEO and National Archivist

Prof. Charles J. Farrugia

National Archives
Chief Executive Officer and National Archivist of Malta since 2005. A Commonwealth Scholar, holds an Honours Degree and Master's Degree in history from the University of Malta, a Postgraduate Diploma in Records Management from the University of Northumbria at Newcastle, an M.A. in Archives and Records Management from University College London, and a PhD from the University of Aberystwyth. Drafted the National Archives of Malta Act (2005) and led the separation of archives from libraries into a standalone National Archives Agency. Served nine years as Chairman of the Association of Commonwealth Archivists and Records Managers (ACARM) and sits on the Executive Board of EURBICA. Also Head of Department and Senior Lecturer within the University of Malta's Faculty of Media and Knowledge Sciences.
