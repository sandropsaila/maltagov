Hon. Dr Clifton Grima

Ministry for Justice, Research and Innovation

Hon. Dr Clifton Grima is a Maltese lawyer and politician who was appointed Minister for Justice, Research and Innovation following the June 2026 Cabinet formation, moving from the education brief he had held since 2021. He graduated as a Doctor of Laws from the University of Malta in 2010, was elected Mayor of Msida in 2009 (serving until 2013), and served as CEO of Mount Carmel Hospital under Prime Minister Joseph Muscat.

He was first elected to Parliament in October 2016 (District 9) and, following the June 2017 general election, was appointed Parliamentary Secretary for Youth, Sport and Voluntary Organisations. In December 2021 he became Minister for Education, and his portfolio subsequently expanded to Education, Youth, Sport, Research and Innovation, during which he steered curriculum reform, technology integration and educational infrastructure investment, including the 2023–2027 Sectoral Agreement with the Malta Union of Teachers.

As Justice Minister, Dr Grima oversees the Courts of Justice, the Office of the Attorney General, the Office of the State Advocate, data protection and freedom of information, and Malta's research and innovation and space strategy portfolios, including Xjenza Malta. He has engaged with the Council of Europe's Venice Commission on reforms to strengthen the independence of specialised tribunals, launched Xjenza Malta's Transdisciplinary Research Programme, and signed a Mediterranean research cooperation agreement with Italy's OGS in June 2026.
