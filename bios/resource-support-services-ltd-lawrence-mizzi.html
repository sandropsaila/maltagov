Chairperson & CEO

Lawrence Mizzi

Resource Support & Services Ltd
Office of the Prime Minister (OPM)
Lawrence Mizzi, 73, is Chairperson and Chief Executive Officer of Resource Support and Services Limited (RSSL), a government-owned company under the Office of the Prime Minister set up in 2003 following the restructuring of Malta Shipyards Limited, tasked with employing surplus workers from government-controlled entities and redeploying them across the public sector. He is the father of former Minister Konrad Mizzi. His continued dual role as both Chair and CEO was confirmed in a December 2025 National Audit Office report, which also flagged governance shortcomings at RSSL including a lack of a formal mission statement and weak payroll controls, with the company employing 629 people as of 2024.
