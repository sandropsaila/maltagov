Commissioner

Ms Renee Laiviera

National Commission for the Promotion of Equality
Commissioner of the National Commission for the Promotion of Equality (NCPE) and NCPE's Acting Executive Director. Has held management positions in the Malta Public Service since 1998, when she was appointed Director (Women's Rights). Served as Executive Member of the European Women's Lobby and Chairperson of the Malta Confederation of Women's Organisations until May 2013. Honoured with the WEF Award 2021 - Women of the Decade in Gender Equality by the Malta-India Business Council of the Women's Indian Chamber of Commerce & Industry (WICCI). NCPE is legally headed by a Commissioner and seven Commission members under Chapter 456 of the Laws of Malta; a Bill to replace NCPE with a new National Commission for Human Rights and Equality collapsed in Parliament in July 2026.
