Prime Minister of Malta

Hon. Dr Robert Abela

Office of the Prime Minister

Hon. Dr Robert Abela is a Maltese lawyer and politician who has served as the 14th Prime Minister of Malta since 13 January 2020, and as Leader of the Labour Party since 12 January 2020. He was born in Sliema on 7 December 1977, the son of former President George Abela. He received his primary education at St Francis School and his secondary and sixth-form education at St Aloysius' College, graduating as a lawyer from the University of Malta in 2002. Before entering politics he worked in the family law firm and acted as legal consultant to various public entities and trade unions.

He was elected to Parliament in June 2017 and appointed legal adviser to the Cabinet of Ministers. Following Joseph Muscat's resignation, Abela became Prime Minister in January 2020. He led Labour to general election victories in 2022 and again on 30 May 2026, securing a historic fourth consecutive mandate and forming a new Cabinet — one of the largest in Malta's history — which took office on 1 June 2026.

Dr Abela's government has centred on Malta Vision 2050, the country's first comprehensive long-term national strategy, alongside continued economic growth, and projects such as the return of Manoel Island, Fort Tigné and White Rocks to public access. He is married to Lydia Abela (née Zerafa) and they have one daughter.
