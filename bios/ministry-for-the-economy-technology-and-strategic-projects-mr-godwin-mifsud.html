Permanent Secretary — Ministry for the Economy, Technology and Strategic Projects

Mr Godwin Mifsud

Ministry for the Economy, Technology and Strategic Projects

Mr Godwin Mifsud is Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for the Economy, Technology and Strategic Projects, appointed in June 2026. He joined the Malta Public Service in 2001 as an Economics Officer, serving at the Economic Policy Department within the Ministry for Finance in various capacities, before his appointment as Director (Structural Economic Research) in 2011 and subsequently Director General (Economic Policy) in 2015, where he consolidated the Department's policy advisory function with a focus on empirical evidence.

He has represented Malta in high-level international fora, including as Malta's Head of Delegation to the Economic Policy Committee, one of the preparatory bodies of the ECOFIN Council, and as Chairman of the Ageing Populations and Sustainability Working Group of the same committee. He has led delegations in meetings with the European Commission, the International Monetary Fund, the World Bank and credit rating agencies.

Mr Mifsud was appointed Permanent Secretary within the Ministry for Transport, Infrastructure and Capital Projects in September 2022, taking on responsibility for the Public Works Department in January 2024. In January 2025 he was appointed Permanent Secretary within the Ministry for the Economy, Enterprise and Strategic Projects, and in June 2026 he was appointed to his current role at the renamed Ministry for the Economy, Technology and Strategic Projects. He holds post-graduate degrees in Economics and Public Policy from institutions in Malta and the United Kingdom, and sits on several Government boards and committees.
