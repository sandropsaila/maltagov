Chairman (Government-nominated)

Dr Gordon Cordina

Bank of Valletta (Government representatives)
Chairman and Non-Executive Director of Bank of Valletta p.l.c., originally nominated by the Maltese government in 2020 (appointment effective October 2020, following a cooling-off period from his prior directorship at HSBC Malta). A leading Maltese economist with 25+ years' experience spanning banking, policymaking, and academia, he previously served as manager of the Research Department at the Central Bank of Malta, Director General of the National Statistics Office, and head of the Economics Department at the University of Malta. He co-founded a private economic consultancy in 2006 and remains a visiting senior lecturer at the University of Malta. Confirmed still serving as Chairman alongside CEO Kenneth Farrugia at BOV's 52nd Annual General Meeting, June 2026.
