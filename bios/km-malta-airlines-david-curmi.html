Executive Chairman

David Curmi

KM Malta Airlines
Executive Chairman of KM Malta Airlines, the successor to Air Malta which he also chaired from 2021 until its closure in March 2024. A former President of the Malta Chamber of Commerce, Enterprise and Industry and an Associate of the Chartered Insurance Institute of the United Kingdom (Chartered Insurer). Appointed by Finance Minister Clyde Caruana; his contract, signed 1 December 2023, was revealed via a Freedom of Information ruling to pay him €260,000 a year, roughly five times the Finance Minister's own package. Also sits on the board of Centrecom Ltd, a company partly owned by KM Malta Airlines.
