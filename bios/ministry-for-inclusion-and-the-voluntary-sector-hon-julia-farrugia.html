Hon. Julia Farrugia

Ministry for Inclusion and the Voluntary Sector

Hon. Julia Farrugia (also known as Julia Farrugia Portelli) is a Maltese politician and former journalist who has served as Minister for Inclusion and the Voluntary Sector since 2022, reconfirmed in the role following the June 2026 Cabinet formation. She was first elected to Parliament in the 2017 general election from District 5 and served as Parliamentary Secretary for Reforms, Citizenship and Simplification of Administrative Processes within the Office of the Prime Minister from 2017 to 2020, working on human trafficking, constitutional reform and the VOTE16 reform that lowered Malta's voting age to 16 — making Malta the second EU member state to do so.

She was appointed Minister for Tourism and Consumer Protection in January 2020. As Minister for Inclusion, she launched Malta's first National Strategy for the Rights of Persons with Disability and the National Strategy for People on the Autism Spectrum, and led changes to the Criminal Code making hate speech towards people with disability a criminal offence. She is a former journalist with 20 years' experience across television, radio and print, and was the first female editor of a Maltese newspaper. In 2018 she received the "Impactful Politics" award from JCI Malta.
