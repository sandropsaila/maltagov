Permanent Secretary — Ministry for Equality and Civil Rights

Mr Emanuel Psaila

Ministry for Equality and Civil Rights

Mr Emanuel Psaila is Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Equality and Civil Rights, appointed in June 2026. He was born in 1968 in Marsaxlokk but grew up mainly in Żejtun. He joined the Public Service in 1986, working after a two-year apprenticeship in the restoration section of the Valletta Rehabilitation Project.

He joined the Civil Protection Department upon its establishment in 1999, serving 16 years as Assistance and Rescue Officer before moving into the department's administration as Public Relations Manager, then Deputy Director. He became the fifth Director of the Civil Protection Department in 2017 and Director General three years later. He served for 14 years as President of the Civil Protection Association, and has sat on the Academy for Disciplined Forces and the National Book Council.

In April 2022 Mr Psaila was appointed Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Home Affairs, Security, Reforms and Equality, and in September 2023 became Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Social and Affordable Accommodation. He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Maltese and a Master's in Maltese Literature, and his creative writing has received literary awards. In 2021 he was named Manager of the Year in the National Worker of the Year Award. He is married with a daughter.
