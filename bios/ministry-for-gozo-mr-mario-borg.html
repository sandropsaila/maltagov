Permanent Secretary — Ministry for Gozo

Mr Mario Borg

Ministry for Gozo

Mr Mario Borg is Permanent Secretary at the Ministry for Gozo, appointed in May 2025 (one of three Permanent Secretaries announced that month, alongside Renzo De Gabriele and Ronald Mizzi) and retained in the role following the June 2026 Cabinet formation. Born in 1975, he began his career in the Civil Service in 1994 as a clerk at the VAT Department.

In 2004 he joined the Economic Policy Department within the Ministry for Finance, initially as an Economic Officer and later as an Economic Analyst. In 2015 he was appointed Director of Economic and Business Statistics at the National Statistics Office (NSO). In 2020 he became the first Chief Executive Officer of the Gozo Regional Development Authority (GRDA), a role he held until his Permanent Secretary appointment.

Throughout his career Mr Borg has been actively engaged in bilateral discussions with the European Commission and international institutions such as the IMF, World Bank and credit rating agencies, and represented Malta on technical working groups within DG ECFIN and Eurostat. He has served on Malta's National Blockchain Taskforce, the Board of Governors of the MFSA, and the Planning Board, and has published papers on socio-economic issues. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce and an MA in Island and Small States from the University of Malta, and an MSc in Public Economics from the University of York, UK.
