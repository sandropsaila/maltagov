Member

Joseph Azzopardi

Financial Services Tribunal
Member of the Malta Financial Services Tribunal (MFST), appointed 2015. Fellow of the Chartered Association of Certified Accountants (FCCA), holds a practising warrant in auditing, and is a graduate MBA from Warwick Business School. Educated at Stella Maris College as a scholarship student. Worked at a Big Five audit firm in Malta and overseas, lectured at the University of Malta's Department of Management, and served as an advisory partner in a mid-tier audit firm. Was a Director of the Malta Stock Exchange from 2013 to 2015, chairing its audit committee, and co-authored 'A Director's Guidebook to the Companies Act 1995' in 1996.
