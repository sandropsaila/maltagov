Chairman

Dr Ian J. Stafrace

Financial Services Tribunal
Chairman of the Malta Financial Services Tribunal (MFST). A Maltese lawyer admitted to the Bar in 1998, with a career spanning corporate matters, mergers, divisions and company restructuring. Served as Chief Executive Officer of the Malta Environment and Planning Authority (MEPA), the predecessor to the Planning Authority and Environment and Resources Authority. Since May 2013 has run his own firm, Salibastafrace Legal, advising public and private entities on property, civil and commercial matters.
