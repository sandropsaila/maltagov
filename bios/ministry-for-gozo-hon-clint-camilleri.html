Hon. Perit Clint Camilleri

Ministry for Gozo

Hon. Perit Clint Camilleri is a Maltese architect, civil engineer and politician who has served as Minister for Gozo since January 2020, retained in the role following the June 2026 Cabinet formation (with planning responsibility for Gozo shifting to the national Infrastructure, Planning and Employment portfolio under Jonathan Attard around the same time). He was born on 24 February 1988 in Gozo to Anton Camilleri, a former Qala local councillor and mayor, and Marlene (née Gauci). He studied at the University of Malta and the University of Kentucky.

He served as Mayor of Qala from 2012 to 2017. Elected to Parliament in June 2017, he served as Parliamentary Secretary for Agriculture, Fisheries and Animal Rights from 2017 to January 2020. In January 2020 he was briefly appointed Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries, Animal Rights and Consumer Protection before being reassigned days later to Minister for Gozo, following Justyne Caruana's resignation amid the fallout of the Yorgen Fenech affair; Anton Refalo took over the Agriculture portfolio in the same reshuffle.

As Gozo Minister, Camilleri has overseen the island's economic recovery and infrastructure programmes, and between January 2024 and the June 2026 reshuffle also held responsibility for planning as Minister for Gozo and Planning. He is married to Dr Deborah Camilleri (née Mercieca) and has two children. His sister, Francesca Camilleri Vettiger, is Malta's Permanent Representative to the Council of Europe.
