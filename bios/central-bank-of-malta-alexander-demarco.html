Governor and Chairman

Alexander Demarco

Central Bank of Malta
Governor and Chairman of the Central Bank of Malta, appointed for a five-year term effective 1 January 2026, succeeding Edward Scicluna. Served as Deputy Governor from 1 January 2014, initially responsible for Financial Stability and, from August 2017, for Monetary Policy; also served as Acting Governor between 1 August 2024 and 31 July 2025, during which he chaired the Joint Financial Stability Board. Joined the Central Bank of Malta in 1984, spending most of his career in the Economic Research Department. Graduated B.A. (Hons) in Economics from the University of Malta and holds a Master of Science in Economics with distinction from the University of Warwick, UK.
