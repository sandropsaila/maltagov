CEO

Dr Stephen Zammit

Karen Grech Rehabilitation Hospital
Qualified MD (Malta) in 1985. Specialist in Rehabilitation Medicine since 1991; Consultant in Rehabilitation Medicine since 2008. CEO of Karin Grech Rehabilitation Hospital since 2014. National Manager representing Malta in the UEMS (European Union of Medical Specialists) Physical and Rehabilitation Medicine section; member of its Examination Committee and of the Executive Committee of the European Society of PRM; past Vice President of the Mediterranean Forum of PRM.
