Board

Copyright Board

Ministry for the Economy, Technology and Strategic Projects (METP)
Chairman
Dr Ivan Sammut
Legal basis
Article 45, Copyright Act

Role & Objective

The Copyright Board is the statutory body established under Article 45 of the Copyright Act responsible for authorising and regulating Collecting Societies in Malta — the organisations that license and administer copyright royalties on behalf of creators — and approving their royalty tariffs. Chairman Dr Ivan Sammut also serves as Chairperson of the Tourism Appeals Board under the Ministry for Tourism.
Research note
One board Member seat is confirmed (Mr Frederick Cutajar); one further Member seat and the Secretary role are listed as currently vacant on the Board's official page.
