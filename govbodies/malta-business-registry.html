Role & Objective

The Malta Business Registry (MBR) is the state agency responsible for the registration of commercial partnerships in Malta, and for maintaining the statutory registers that underpin corporate transparency and good governance. Its core functions include:
  • Company registration — incorporating and registering companies and other commercial partnerships under the Companies Act (Cap. 386);
  • Register of Beneficial Owners — administering Malta's beneficial ownership disclosure regime, requiring companies to identify and declare the natural persons who ultimately own or control 25% or more of shares/voting rights, with updates required within 14 days of any change and annual confirmation on each registration anniversary;
  • Insolvency oversight — playing a regulatory role in matters of corporate insolvency;
  • Enforcement — the Registrar has powers to refuse registration, restrict new incorporations, and impose administrative penalties (up to €100,000 per officer) for beneficial-ownership non-compliance.
The MBR represents Malta internationally in the business-registry space — it hosted and opened the EBRA Malta 2026 conference (European Business Registries Association) in May 2026, bringing together registrars from Companies House UK, the Danish Business Registry, and others to discuss registry digitalisation and governance.
