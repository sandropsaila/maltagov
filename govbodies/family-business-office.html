Role & Objective

The Family Business Office is the statutory body established under the Family Business Act (2017) to assist Malta's family-owned enterprises with succession planning, governance, and internal organisation. It is headed by a statutory "Regulator," currently Dr Joseph Gerada.
Family businesses are estimated to make up around 75% of all Maltese businesses (per a 2022 Malta Chamber of Commerce survey), making intergenerational transition a matter of significant economic importance. The Office, in collaboration with Malta Enterprise, supports these businesses through:
  • Succession planning support — assisting family businesses in planning leadership transitions to the next generation;
  • Governance advisory — helping family enterprises establish appropriate governance structures to separate family and business decision-making;
  • Incentive schemes — administering financial incentives including the Family Businesses Grant Scheme and the SME Guarantee Scheme.
