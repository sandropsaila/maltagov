Role & Objective

SmartCity Malta is a 360,000 square-metre business and technology park adjacent to Valletta's Grand Harbour, developed as a joint venture between the Government of Malta (9% equity) and Dubai Holding/TECOM. It is one of Malta's flagship dedicated business-park developments, hosting technology, media, and knowledge-economy tenants in a purpose-built campus setting.
Governance note
Unlike most entities in this register, SmartCity Malta's Chairman and CEO are Dubai Holding appointees, not government appointees — the Government of Malta holds a board seat only by virtue of its minority (9%) equity stake. This entity is included in the register because of the Government's equity interest, but its day-to-day leadership answers to Dubai Holding.
