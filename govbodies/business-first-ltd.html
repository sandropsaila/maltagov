Role & Objective

Business First (Business 1st) is Malta's one-stop-shop for business, providing a single point of contact for enterprises navigating government services, licensing, permits, and business-support schemes. It complements Malta Enterprise's incentive-focused mandate with a more operational, administrative-navigation role — helping businesses of all sizes cut through bureaucracy when setting up, expanding, or complying with regulatory requirements.
CEO Marika Tonna also serves as a member of the Commission on Gender-Based Violence and Domestic Violence, reflecting a broader public-service profile beyond her Business First role.
