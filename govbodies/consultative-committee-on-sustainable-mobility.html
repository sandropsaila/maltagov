Established

Chairperson
Matthew Farrugia (also Ambassador for Sustainable Mobility)

Role & Objective

The Consultative Committee on Sustainable Mobility is an advisory body attached to the Ministry for Sustainable Mobility, created to steer Malta's transition toward more sustainable, lower-emission, and more inclusive forms of transport. Its official remit, as set out at appointment, is to:
  • advise government on sustainable transport projects;
  • promote innovation and social inclusion in mobility policy;
  • assist in integrating mobility considerations into urban planning;
  • facilitate collaboration between the various stakeholders involved in transport (ministries, transport agencies, academia, NGOs and local government);
  • evaluate progress on sustainable-mobility initiatives; and
  • recommend measures to improve air quality and access to transport.
The Committee sits within the wider "Reshaping Our Mobility" programme, an 18-month government initiative that goes beyond car- and motorbike-specific incentives to address alternative mobility more broadly — including the first National Cycling Strategy. Its Chairperson, Matthew Farrugia, doubles as Malta's Ambassador for Sustainable Mobility, a role focused on public engagement, workshops, and representing the interests of vulnerable transport users (e.g. cyclists, pedestrians, and those without private cars).
Structurally, the Committee is a cross-institutional coordination forum rather than an implementing agency: it draws representatives from the Ministry for Transport/Infrastructure/Public Works, the Ministry for Environment, Energy and Public Cleanliness, the Ministry for Gozo and Planning, Transport Malta, Infrastructure Malta, academic institutions, NGOs, and the Department of Local Government — pooling policy, planning, and delivery perspectives on mobility in one advisory table.

Deloitte Malta — Potential Service Opportunities

Illustrative fit analysis — not a confirmed engagement or relationship
Sustainable Urban Mobility Plan (SUMP) & Transport Strategy Advisory
Strategy & Transactions — Infrastructure & Real Estate
The Committee's mandate to integrate mobility into urban planning and recommend transport-access measures overlaps directly with Deloitte's infrastructure planning and transport-strategy work. Deloitte Malta could support scenario modelling, prioritisation frameworks, and delivery roadmaps for SUMP-aligned initiatives (Malta already has EU Technical Support Instrument-funded SUMP/SULP work underway for the Valletta region), and for the National Transport Masterplan 2030.
Climate & Decarbonisation Strategy for Transport
Sustainability & Climate
Given the Committee's brief to improve air quality and cut transport emissions, Deloitte's Climate & Decarbonisation service line (carbon-footprint baselining, transition-pathway design, target setting) could support quantifying the emissions impact of proposed mobility measures and benchmarking Malta's approach against EU Fit for 55 and Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Regulation obligations.
EU Funding & Grants Structuring
Tax — Government Grants, Credits & Incentives
Sustainable-mobility programmes in Malta are heavily EU co-financed (Cohesion Fund, RRF, Technical Support Instrument). Deloitte's grants advisory practice could help the Committee's stakeholder ministries and agencies structure, apply for, and report on EU funding streams tied to alternative mobility, electromobility, and active-travel infrastructure.
Multi-Stakeholder Governance & Programme Management Office (PMO)
Human Capital — Organisation & Work Transformation
The Committee's core function is coordinating across ministries, agencies, academia and NGOs. Deloitte's organisational-design and change-management capability could support a formal PMO or governance model for the Committee itself, clarifying decision rights, reporting lines, and stakeholder-engagement cadences across the "Reshaping Our Mobility" programme.
Mobility Data & Analytics Platform
Engineering, AI & Data
Evaluating progress and recommending access/air-quality measures both depend on reliable mobility data (e.g. the National Household Travel Survey, traffic and public-transport usage data). Deloitte's data-engineering and AI capability could help build a dashboard or analytics layer to track KPIs feeding the Committee's evaluation function.
Regulatory & Policy Advisory
Risk, Regulatory & Forensic
As alternative-mobility and low-emission-zone policy develops, Deloitte's regulatory advisory practice could support impact assessments and compliance-readiness reviews for new mobility rules (e.g. Controlled Vehicular Access changes, EV-charging mandates) that fall within the Committee's advisory remit.
