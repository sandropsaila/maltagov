Role & Objective

Bank of Valletta (BOV) is Malta's largest listed bank, with the Government of Malta holding a significant minority shareholding. This entry in the register tracks the Government's board-level representation — the government-nominated Chairman/non-executive director seat — rather than BOV as a company in its entirety.
Dr Gordon Cordina was nominated by government as BOV Chairman in 2020 and was confirmed still serving as Chairman/Non-Executive Director at BOV's 52nd Annual General Meeting in June 2026, alongside CEO Kenneth Farrugia. Cordina is a well-known Maltese economist with a long public-facing career in economic commentary and analysis.
