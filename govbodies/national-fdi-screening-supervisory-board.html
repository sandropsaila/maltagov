Role & Objective

The National FDI Screening Supervisory Board (NFDIS Malta) is Malta's national supervisory body implementing the EU's Foreign Direct Investment Screening Regulation (Regulation 2019/452), which entered full effect on 11 October 2020. The framework enables national governments to evaluate and monitor foreign investments in critical sectors — energy, transport, communications, critical technologies, and other strategically sensitive areas — safeguarding EU security and public order while keeping the EU an open investment destination.
As Malta's national screening authority, the Board reviews qualifying foreign investments, cooperates with the European Commission and other Member States through the EU's information-sharing mechanism, and has the final say on whether a specific investment operation should be permitted on Maltese territory. The Board's chairperson, Dr Geraldine Spiteri Lucas, also serves as CEO of the Malta Business Registry — reflecting the natural institutional overlap between corporate-registry oversight and FDI screening.
The EU framework is currently being strengthened: a revised Regulation, provisionally agreed by the European Parliament, Council and Commission in December 2025, will mandate FDI screening mechanisms in all Member States, broaden the sectoral scope, extend screening to indirect foreign control via intra-EU investors, and introduce new EU-level digital filing tools.
