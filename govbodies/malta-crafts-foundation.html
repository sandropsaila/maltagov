Role & Objective

The Malta Crafts Foundation is a public organisation dedicated to preserving and promoting Maltese artisanal products and skills. It supports the country's craft economy — encompassing traditional trades and skilled artisan production — through preservation of heritage techniques, promotion of local craftspeople, and market-access support.
