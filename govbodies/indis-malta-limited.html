Role & Objective

Indis Malta Ltd (formerly Malta Industrial Parks Ltd) is the largest operator of industrial parks in the Maltese Islands, responsible for administering government-owned industrial estates across Malta and Gozo. It provides the physical infrastructure — factories, artisan villages, life-sciences laboratories, and aviation-cluster facilities — that underpins Malta's manufacturing, life sciences, and advanced-industry base.
The company is currently delivering a €470 million industrial infrastructure investment programme spanning new factory space, artisan villages, life sciences laboratories, and aviation-cluster facilities — directly supporting the manufacturing and life-sciences sectors that Malta Enterprise promotes to foreign investors.
