Role & Objective

Xjenza Malta (Maltese for "Science Malta") is the Government of Malta agency acting as the national R&D and Space funding agency, and the policy development and implementation body for Research, Innovation and Space. It is entrusted with developing and managing Malta's national R&I and space policy frameworks, and with overseeing and coordinating implementation of the national research and space strategy, including direct management of the national public budgetary allocation for Research, Innovation and Space. Xjenza Malta was formed through the restructuring and rebranding of the former Malta Council for Science and Technology (MCST) into a fully-fledged government agency mandated by law to regulate Malta's R&I and Space policy and programming efforts.
The Agency's core work spans several areas:
  • Policy & Strategy — implementing the National R&I Strategic Plan (2023–2027), which emphasises bridging the gap between academia and industry;
  • R&I Programmes — administering the FUSION national funding portfolio (basic research, applied research, and commercialisation), which grew from €5 million to €6.3 million under the 2025 budget, and disbursed over €9 million across various programmes in 2024;
  • Internationalisation — since 2019, the Internationalisation Unit has disbursed €7.5 million across 42 international collaboration projects, and manages bilateral R&I agreements (e.g. with Italy, Turkey, China) and Mediterranean partnerships under the PRIMA programme;
  • Framework Programme (Horizon Europe) — Malta's national contact point function for the EU's flagship R&I funding programme;
  • Space — implementing Malta's National Space Strategy (2022–2027); Malta became an ESA PECS (Plan for European Cooperating States) partner in 2024, securing roughly €1.5 million annually for five years, and Xjenza Malta joined the International Astronautical Federation in 2024 and the European Space Policy Institute (ESPI) in 2025.
Xjenza Malta also directly operates Esplora, Malta's national interactive science centre and planetarium, and manages Villa Bighi in Kalkara, its headquarters and event venue.
Leadership scope note
Xjenza Malta is an executive government agency with an operational management team rather than a politically-appointed governance board. This profile captures the CEO and the three Senior Directors who head the Agency's principal functional pillars (Corporate Services, Esplora, and Strategy/Research/Technical Affairs); the Agency's website also lists 11 Directors and 12 Deputy Directors managing specific programme areas, who are not individually profiled here as operational staff rather than named appointees.
