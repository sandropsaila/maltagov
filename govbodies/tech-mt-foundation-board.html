Role & Objective

Tech.mt is a public–private partnership launched in 2019 by the Government of Malta and the Malta Chamber of Commerce, Enterprise and Industry to promote Malta's National Strategy on Innovative Technology. It positions itself as the country's gateway to the tech industry, working to establish Malta as a creative, tech-savvy nation that attracts foreign direct investment while helping local tech companies expand internationally.
The Foundation organises its work around four stated objectives:
  • Promote — giving local tech companies a platform at local and international symposia to showcase their work;
  • Innovate — exposing tech professionals to innovative technology and driving academic research, and helping display that research to the right audiences;
  • Talent — building a two-way communication channel between local and foreign academia to identify areas of collaboration;
  • Assist — providing market intelligence via research and publications, facilitating information on investment incentives, and mentoring tech-company business development.
In practice this covers running flagship international outreach events (e.g. inMalta at London Tech Week, GITEX AI Europe), maintaining a members directory and corporate-membership scheme, publishing financial and market reports, and helping local tech firms access financial instruments and grants.
Governance sits at two levels: an Executive Committee (Chairman, Deputy Chairman, CEO) responsible for overall performance and strategic implementation, and a wider Board of Governors — appointed jointly by the Government of Malta and the Malta Chamber of Commerce — responsible for attaining the Foundation's objectives.
