Role & Objective

The Co-operatives Board (Bord tal-Koperattivi) is the statutory body responsible for registering, monitoring and supervising co-operative societies in Malta under the Co-operative Societies Act. Co-operatives are member-owned businesses operating across sectors including agriculture, fisheries, housing, and consumer goods, and the Board ensures they comply with statutory governance and financial-reporting obligations.
