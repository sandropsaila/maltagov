Licensing Appeals Board (Trading)

Board Secretary

Legal basis
Trade Licensing Act

Role & Objective

The Trade Licensing Appeals Board hears appeals from decisions made by the Trade Licensing Unit and Local Councils under the Trade Licensing Act. It provides a formal review mechanism for businesses and traders who wish to contest licensing decisions affecting their commercial operations.
