Role & Objective

The Central Business District Foundation (formerly the Mrieħel Enterprise Zone Foundation) is a public-private partnership between the Government of Malta and private-sector operators, established in 2016 to regenerate the Mrieħel business district into a modern Central Business District. The Foundation drives infrastructure upgrades, urban-planning coordination, and stakeholder engagement to transform what was historically an industrial/light-manufacturing zone into a mixed-use commercial and office district.
Chairman William Wait concurrently chairs Malta Enterprise, reflecting the close policy overlap between Malta's investment-promotion agency and its flagship business-district regeneration project.
Research note
