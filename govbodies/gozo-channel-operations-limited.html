Role & Objective

Gozo Channel (Operations) Limited operates the ferry service connecting Malta and Gozo, a critical piece of national transport infrastructure linking the two main islands. The company underwent a significant governance restructuring in July 2026, separating the Chairperson and CEO roles for the first time and making the CEO role a full-time executive position, with the new leadership's mandate explicitly focused on fleet modernisation, Mġarr Harbour upgrades, and digital transformation.
Chairperson Marlene Cini brings extensive experience in public administration, legal affairs and corporate governance, having previously served as Board Secretary of Gozo Channel Operations Ltd itself, Board Secretary of the Gozo Regional Development Authority, and Vice Head of Malta's delegation in an EU body — giving her deep institutional continuity heading into the restructuring.
