Role & Objective

Malta Government Technology Investments Ltd (MGTIL) is a Government-owned company tasked with seeking business investment opportunities in technology, spanning tech startups, digital infrastructure, green energy, and national security systems. It functions as a strategic-investment vehicle for the Maltese state in the technology sector, distinct from Malta Government Venture Capital Ltd's more traditional startup-equity focus.
