Role & Objective

Malta Enterprise is Malta's economic development agency, tasked with attracting new foreign direct investment as well as facilitating the growth of existing operations. It is the country's primary interface between government and industry for investment promotion, operating across sectors including advanced manufacturing, aviation, education, healthcare, ICT and shared services, life sciences, maritime, and visual arts.
The Agency's core functions include:
  • Investment promotion — internationally marketing Malta's economic climate, tax regime, and business-set-up process to prospective investors;
  • Support schemes — administering a wide range of incentive schemes (grants, tax credits, and combinations of both) for business development, expansion, R&D, environmental performance, and digital transformation, including the Business Development Scheme (capped at €300,000 per undertaking over a rolling three-year period) and the INVEST scheme for initial investment projects;
  • EU affairs coordination — engaging with EU-level industrial policy initiatives including the Net-Zero Industry Act (NZIA), the Critical Raw Materials Act (CRMA), and the Chips Joint Undertaking (semiconductors), and operating the Malta Semiconductor Competence Centre;
  • Business First — a one-stop-shop service supporting businesses of all sizes with planning and growth.
Malta Enterprise is governed by a Board of Directors under a Chairman, with day-to-day operations led by a Chief Executive Officer.
